## 1. REFUND_POLICY

```html
<h4 style="text-align: center;">Refund, Replacement &amp; Cancellation Policy</h4>
<p>At {{storeName}}, each product is made to order and produced specifically for you after the order is placed. Because of this, we maintain a strict but fair refund and replacement policy.</p>
<p><strong>1. Order Cancellation</strong></p>
<p>Orders may be cancelled <strong>within 6 hours of placing the order.</strong><br>After this period, the order may already be in production and cannot be cancelled or modified.<br>To request a cancellation, please contact us immediately at: {{email}}</p>
<p>Requests submitted after the 6-hour cancellation window cannot be guaranteed as the order may already be in production.</p>
<p>If a cancellation request is approved within the allowed time window, a 30% management, processing, and transaction fee will be deducted from the total order value.<br>This fee covers costs that are non-recoverable once the order is placed, including but not limited to:<br>- Payment processing and transaction fees<br>- Order processing and administrative costs<br>- Production preparation and fulfillment system allocation<br>The remaining balance will be refunded to the original payment method.</p>
<p><strong>2. Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items</strong></p>
<p>If you receive an item that is <strong>defective, damaged during shipping, or incorrect</strong>, please contact us within <strong>72 hours after delivery confirmation.</strong><br>Your email must include:<br>- Your order number<br>- <strong>Clear photos</strong> of the product and the issue<br>- A brief description of the problem</p>
<p>Please send your request to: {{email}}</p>
<p>After review and approval, we will <strong>either</strong>:<br>- Send a <strong>free replacement</strong>, or<br>- Issue a <strong>full refund</strong></p>
<p>We may request additional photos or information to verify the issue.<br>In some cases, we may request the defective item to be returned before processing the resolution.</p>
<p><strong>3. Custom &amp; Made-To-Order Products</strong></p>
<p>All products sold on {{storeName}} are <strong>custom-made or printed on demand.</strong><br>Because these products are made specifically for each order, <strong>we do not accept returns or refunds for the following reasons:</strong><br>- Incorrect size ordered<br>- Change of mind<br>- Incorrect shipping address provided by the customer<br>- Minor color differences due to screen display<br>- Custom name or personalization errors provided by the customer</p>
<p>However, we understand mistakes happen. As a courtesy, we may offer <strong>a replacement with a 50% discount</strong> if you wish to reorder the correct size, color, or product.</p>
<p><strong>4. Refund Processing</strong></p>
<p>If your refund request is approved, refunds will be processed to your <strong>original payment method.</strong><br>Please allow <strong>5–7 business days</strong> for the refund to appear in your account depending on your bank or payment provider.<br>Once a refund has been issued, we will send a <strong>confirmation email.</strong></p>
<p><strong>5. Late or Missing Refunds</strong></p>
<p>If you have not received your refund:<br>- Check your bank account again.<br>- Contact your credit card company or payment provider.<br>- Contact your bank.</p>
<p>Refunds can take several business days to process depending on your financial institution.<br>If 10 business days have passed since the refund confirmation and you still have not received the funds, please contact us at: {{email}}</p>
<p><strong>6. Chargebacks &amp; Payment Disputes</strong></p>
<p>If a chargeback or payment dispute is opened without contacting our support team first, we reserve the right to submit all order information, delivery confirmation, and communication records to the payment provider.<br>We are always happy to resolve any issues directly with our customers and encourage contacting us first for the fastest resolution.</p>
```

## 2. SHIPPING_POLICY

```html
<p><strong>Production Time</strong></p>
<p>All products at {{storeName}} are made-to-order and produced specifically according to your selected design, size, and color. We do not keep inventory, which means each item is manufactured after your order is placed.<br>Production typically takes 5–10 business days (excluding weekends and holidays) before the order is shipped.<br>Orders enter the production process within 6 hours after being placed. Once production has started, orders cannot be modified or canceled.</p>
<p><strong>Shipping Time</strong></p>
<p>After production is completed, your order will be shipped according to the shipping method selected at checkout.</p>
<p>Estimated delivery times:<br>Standard Shipping: 10–20 business days<br>Fast Shipping: 5–10 business days</p>
<p>Delivery times may vary depending on your location, customs processing, and carrier delays.<br>Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email.</p>
<p><strong>Estimated Delivery Time</strong></p>
<p>Total estimated delivery time includes production time + shipping time.<br>Example:<br>- Standard Shipping: 15–30 business days total<br>- Fast Shipping: 10–20 business days total</p>
<p>For the most accurate estimate, please check the estimated delivery date on your order confirmation email.<br>If you have any questions about your order, please contact us at: {{email}}</p>
<p><strong>Shipping Cost</strong></p>
<p>- $5.95 for orders under $90<br>- Free shipping for orders $90 and above</p>
<p><strong>{{storeName}} is not responsible for delays caused by carriers, customs clearance, weather conditions, or peak holiday seasons.</strong></p>
```
